# Source escrow: DRAFT TERMS, NO AGENT ENGAGED

**Status:** offer text drafted; no escrow agent engaged and no agreement
signed.

## The offer

For enterprise customers who require it, ReqPub will place the application
source, the database schema and migrations, the verification tool, and the
build instructions with a mutually agreed escrow agent, with release triggered
by cessation of business or material uncured breach.

## What makes this offer smaller than it usually is

Most escrow arrangements exist because a customer cannot read their own data if
the vendor disappears. That is not the situation here. The record formats are
published, the verification tool is standalone and dependency-free, and an
evidence pack verifies with `openssl` and twenty lines of code. A customer who
exports evidence packs regularly already holds everything needed to prove what
was agreed, without ReqPub and without an escrow agent.

Escrow covers continuity of the running service. It is not, and should not be
sold as, the answer to the durability of the records themselves. That answer is
the offline verification walkthrough.

## Decisions required
