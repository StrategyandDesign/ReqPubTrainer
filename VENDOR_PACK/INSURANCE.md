# Vendor insurance: OWNER ITEM O4, NOT OBTAINED

**Status:** no errors-and-omissions or cyber liability policy is in place. A
certificate of insurance will replace this file.

Most Fortune 100 vendor questionnaires ask for E&O and cyber liability with
named limits, and several require the buyer to be listed as an additional
insured. This is typically the longest-lead item in an enterprise onboarding,
often longer than the penetration test, and it is stated first here for that
reason.

## Decisions required

Carrier and broker. Limits, per occurrence and aggregate. Whether the policy
covers professional services delivered through the platform or only the
platform. Additional-insured endorsement process and turnaround.
