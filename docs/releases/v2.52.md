# RELEASE REPORT v2.52: The evidence pack

One artifact a lawyer, an auditor, or a carrier can hold: what was agreed,
what changed, who signed, provable, offline. Read-only, manager-gated,
inert until exported.

## What shipped

One definer RPC, evidence_gather, returns the whole record as one jsonb:
chronology with meta omitted and the omission stated per standing decision
D2, versions with their snapshots, signature facts with the signer's
domain and never the address, receipt facts with their timestamps,
the attachments manifest, the referenced public keys, and the chain
verification verbatim. One RPC so the leak assertions have one throat to
test, and they test it: the seeded sign token, the seeded address, and the
poisoned meta all stayed inside.

app/js/evidencepack.js builds the pack pure: README, printable cover,
chronology.json and .csv, one baseline bundle per version through the
published recipe, every sealed receipt exactly as buildReceiptBundle emits
it, attachments-manifest.csv, chain-verification.json, evidence.csv with
the frozen fifteen-column order, the VERIFY excerpt, and manifest.json
hashing every other file over exact bytes. generatedAt lives only in the
manifest; two exports of an unchanged record differ in exactly one file.
Every csv cell that could execute in a spreadsheet is prefixed. The
finance line ships in the README: mapping to revenue recognition
judgments belongs to the firm and its auditors; ReqPub asserts none.

tools/reqpub-verify.mjs gained an evidence mode: strict manifest
verification both ways, every baseline fingerprint reproduced, every
receipt seal verified, the openssl ts commands printed. It still imports
nothing from ReqPub. ../VERIFY.md section 11 is the normative layout
and recipe. One manager action, Export evidence pack, sits on the Version
history panel with a busy state, and the export writes one chained
evidence.exported line: producing evidence is itself on the record.

## Gates

Audit clean: 20 modules, 35 served files, every rpc name present, every
data-action handled, APP_VERSION equals package.json at 2.52.0. Unit
chain green end to end, including 17 evidence builder checks, 6 CLI gate
checks that build a real pack with real Ed25519 on disk and watch the CLI
answer PACK-VERIFIED untouched and MISMATCH after one flipped byte, a
dropped manifest entry, and a stray file. Backend chain exit 0 across all
27 suites, 678 checks, including the 18-check evidence suite: fix applied
twice, manager gathers, viewer refused, rival refused, practice refused
in the doctrine's words, token and address and meta never leaving,
snapshot and timestamp material complete, the export line chained and the
chain verifying over it, and the parity pin that v2.52 adds exactly two
functions, no tables, no triggers.

## Recorded adaptations

The master prompt placed the pack recipe at VERIFY.md section 10; the
document had grown a section since it was written, so the pack is section
11 and every pointer says so. The gather carries version snapshots, the
receipt .tsr fields, and the referenced public keys, which the spec's
field list omitted: the baseline bundles and receipt folders cannot exist
without them, and carrying them keeps the one-throat property intact. The
cover carries no timestamp; the determinism gate caught the first draft
carrying one, and the manifest is the only file that knows the moment of
production. The practice refusal ships now and is exercised in the
embedded suite by adding the practice column there; production stays
inert until v2.55 introduces the column for real. Deployed edge functions
keep reporting the version they were built at until their next deploy;
this release deploys none, so mcp keeps answering 2.51.0 and that is the
honest fact about the running code.
